# composition for empty places and landscape: no figure
a strong leading line pulling the eye from the lower corner into the depth
the horizon on the lower third with a large open sky
perfect symmetry with the vanishing point at the centre
a blurred foreground object framing the lower edge
one small bright detail placed on the right third against a calm field
a high viewpoint looking down across the whole space
a low viewpoint close to the ground with the space rising away
layers of foreground, middle ground and distance, each a different tone
two vertical edges framing the view beyond
the frame filled with repeating shapes and one break in the pattern
a lot of empty space with a single small element off-centre
a diagonal cutting the frame from corner to corner
reflections in water or glass doubling the scene below the horizon
layered planes of foreground, middle ground and background in different tones
repeating shapes across the picture with one deliberate break
one small shape against an enormous open space
