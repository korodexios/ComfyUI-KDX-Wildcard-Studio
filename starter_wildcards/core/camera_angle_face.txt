at eye level, a slight 5 degree tilt
slightly above eye level, tilted down across the brow
slightly below eye level, tilted up along the line of the nose
from a three-quarter viewpoint at cheek height
in a straight profile, level with the bridge of the nose
across one shoulder, the near shoulder soft and out of focus
from above the forehead, the chin slightly lowered in the frame
at mouth height, looking up toward the eye line
level with the cheekbone, from the side
raking from the side, low across the skin
at lash height, level with the eyelashes
tilted 15 degrees to one side
from a high angle at head height, looking down at about 30 degrees
at eye level with the head turned a quarter away
