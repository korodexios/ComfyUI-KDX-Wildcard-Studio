# composition for portrait, selfie and close shots: the face is the main element

the face filling most of the frame with a soft blurred background
the head turned slightly, open space on the side the face is turned toward
one shoulder close to the lens and out of focus
the face off-centre to the left with soft empty space to the right
the head at the exact centre, the background level behind it
the frame crowded, the shoulders close to both edges
the head and shoulders low in the frame with empty space above
the face pushed toward one edge, the other side of the frame left empty
a foreground element blurred across the bottom of the frame
the face framed by a bright shape behind it, edges soft
the frame tilted slightly, the background leaning with it
the face half in shadow, the lit half placed on the left third
the frame crowded, a shoulder close to both edges
