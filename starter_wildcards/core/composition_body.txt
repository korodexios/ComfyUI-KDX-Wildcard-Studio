the body crossing the frame on a diagonal from lower left to upper right
open space left in front of where the subject is looking
the subject pushed into the right corner of the frame, most of the image empty
two vertical edges framing the subject on both sides
one arm reaching toward the camera, the hand the nearest thing in the frame
the subject dead centre, both edges of the frame equally empty
the frame split by a vertical edge, the subject on one side only
a foreground object blurred across the bottom of the frame
the figure pressed against the right edge, empty space filling the left
the subject looking out of the frame toward the empty side
the frame tilted so the floor runs uphill through the picture
the subject framed between two vertical edges, the space beyond them empty
the subject off-centre against the brightest part of the background
the frame tilted a few degrees, the verticals leaning with it
the subject framed in the upper half of the picture
